1. Introduction

RecRem Mail ("the Add-on", "we", "us", or "our") is a Google Workspace add-on designed to help users create, schedule, and manage email reminders, including recurring email reminders, through Google Workspace.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Add-on handles information when you install and use it.

2. Information the Add-on Accesses

Depending on the features you use, the Add-on may access:

  • Your Google account information required for authentication.
  • Google Sheets data that you authorize the Add-on to access.
  • Email addresses used as recipients.
  • Email subject lines and message content entered for reminders.
  • Scheduling dates and times.
  • Recurring reminder settings.
  • Information necessary to record the status of scheduled emails.

The Add-on only accesses information necessary to provide its functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the Add-on is used solely to:

  • Create email reminders.
  • Schedule emails for a specified date and time.
  • Send recurring emails according to the schedule configured by the user.
  • Read information required from an authorized Google Sheet.
  • Update reminder or sending status.
  • Identify and handle failed email-sending operations.
  • Provide and maintain the Add-on's functionality.

4. Google User Data

The Add-on may access Google user data through Google APIs. Google user data is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user.

We do not:

  • Sell Google user data.
  • Rent Google user data.
  • Use Google user data for advertising.
  • Use Google user data to create advertising profiles.
  • Sell or transfer Google user data to data brokers.
  • Use Google user data for purposes unrelated to the Add-on's functionality.

5. Email Content

When a user creates a reminder, the Add-on may process the information necessary to send that email, including:

  • Recipient email address
  • CC/BCC addresses, if provided
  • Email subject
  • Email body
  • Scheduled date and time
  • Recurring schedule

This information is used only to send the email according to the user's instructions.

6. Google Sheets Data

The Add-on may read and write information in Google Sheets when the user authorizes it to do so.

This information may include:

  • Recipient details
  • Email content
  • Scheduling information
  • Recurring schedules
  • Reminder status

The Add-on does not intentionally access unrelated Google Sheets or information that is not necessary for its functionality.

7. Data Storage

The Add-on is designed to use Google Workspace services for processing and storing information required for its operation.

We do not sell or commercially distribute information processed through the Add-on.

Where information is stored within a user's Google account or Google Sheets, that information remains subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information or Google user data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Add-on against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction.

Users are responsible for protecting their Google accounts and controlling access to their Google Sheets and other Google Workspace resources.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

The Add-on retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its functionality.

Information stored in Google Sheets remains under the user's control and can be deleted by the user.

A user may also revoke the Add-on's Google account permissions through their Google Account settings.

11. Third-Party Services

The Add-on may use Google APIs and Google Workspace services to provide its functionality.

The Add-on does not share Google user data with third-party services unless such sharing is necessary for a feature that the user explicitly uses and is disclosed accordingly.

12. Children's Privacy

The Add-on is intended for general productivity and business use and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Add-on.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Add-on's functionality, services, or data practices change.

Any updated Privacy Policy will include a revised effective date.
